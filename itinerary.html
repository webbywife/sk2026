Where it goes

Heads up: in-land transportation is budgeted at $100 for 5 days (~$20/day) — that comfortably covers taxis to Sejong University and AREX airport transfers, with some room for subway top-ups.
Overall: think Manila-summer humidity, not Korean-winter layers. Breathable natural fabrics (cotton, linen), one dressier outfit for Solbam, a compact umbrella, and sunscreen will cover the whole trip. Sneakers over sandals — you'll be walking a lot.
