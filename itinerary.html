Where it goes

Heads up: the Jongno hotel means three extra subway/taxi commutes to COEX (~$1.50–14 each way) that the original plan didn't have. The $100 in-land transport budget still covers it comfortably if you stick to the subway for the daily commute and save taxis for late nights, but it's tighter than before — bump it to ~$120–130 if you'll be taking taxis more often.
